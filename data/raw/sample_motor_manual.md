# MX-200 Induction Motor Maintenance Guide

## Safety

Disconnect and lock out electrical power before inspecting the motor. Verify zero energy state with an approved meter. Allow hot surfaces to cool and wear eye protection. Never operate the motor with guards removed.

## Overheating

Motor overheating can result from overload, restricted ventilation, low or unbalanced supply voltage, excessive ambient temperature, frequent starts, or bearing friction. First compare the measured current on all phases with the nameplate full-load current. A current above the rated value indicates overload or a mechanical restriction.

Inspect the cooling fan, fan cover, and ventilation passages. Remove dust or debris without forcing contamination into the windings. Confirm that the motor has the minimum clearance specified for airflow. Measure phase-to-phase voltage; voltage unbalance greater than one percent can create a much larger current unbalance and excessive heating.

## Vibration

Increased vibration can be caused by loose mounting bolts, shaft misalignment, imbalance, soft foot, damaged bearings, or a driven-load problem. Record vibration at the drive end and non-drive end in horizontal, vertical, and axial directions. Compare the readings with the established machine baseline rather than relying on a single universal threshold.

Inspect mounting bolts and the base before changing alignment. Check coupling alignment after confirming that soft foot is within tolerance. A vibration peak at one times running speed often indicates imbalance, while a strong axial component may indicate misalignment. Bearing defects can produce higher-frequency vibration and may be accompanied by noise or increasing temperature.

## Bearing inspection

Do not add grease until the lubricant type and relubrication interval are confirmed. Over-greasing can increase bearing temperature. Check for abnormal noise, lubricant leakage, discoloration, and excessive radial play. If bearing temperature rises rapidly after lubrication, stop the motor and verify the grease quantity and compatibility.

## Restart checklist

Before restarting, reinstall all guards, remove tools, confirm that personnel are clear, and record the inspection findings. Run the motor unloaded when the process permits. Monitor current, voltage, temperature, and vibration during the first fifteen minutes. Stop the motor if measurements continue to rise or exceed the equipment limits.
